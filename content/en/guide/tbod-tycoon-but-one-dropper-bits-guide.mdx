Even experienced players run into bottlenecks by mismanaging their layouts and upgrade orders. Keep these common community pitfalls in mind:

- **Converting Too Early:** Placing your Bit converter at the start of your conveyor track results in tiny fractions of a Bit. Always place converters after your strongest multipliers.
