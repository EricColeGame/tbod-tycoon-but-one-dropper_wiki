Because you only have one dropper to work with, layout engineering determines your entire cash velocity. As highlighted throughout this **tbod tycoon but one dropper rebirth guide**, ore routing dictates whether your raw materials reach peak multiplier values or get lost to despawn timers.

### The Linear Serpent Route
The serpent layout utilizes alternating conveyor belts to guide ore back and forth across your plot. This ensures every piece of ore touches every available booster without requiring complex logic gates. However, be cautious of conveyor speed upgrades: if your belts run too fast, ores can skip trigger zones on certain compact upgraders.

In higher rebirth tiers, certain upgraders apply special status conditions (such as radioactive, glazed, or electrified) to your ores. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most common mistake made in this tbod tycoon but one dropper rebirth guide?
The most frequent mistake players make is over-investing in late-line upgraders right before reaching their rebirth goal. If your rebirth threshold is $100 Million, spending $80 Million on an extra upgrader when you are already at $70 Million severely delays your reset. Stop buying upgrades the moment you are within 80% of your target threshold.

### Do I lose my unlocked rebirth perks when I reset my plot?
No. Rebirth perks, prestige multipliers, and permanent token unlocks persist through every reset. Only your plot-specific items—such as basic conveyor tracks, purchased mid-tier upgraders, and current liquid cash—are reset back to zero.

### How do I stop my single dropper ores from falling off tracks?
Ores usually tumble off belts when conveyor speed upgrades outpace the turn radius of corner pieces. To resolve this, always place guard rails or slow-down buffer plates right before sharp 90-degree conveyor bends. Keeping your line level and minimizing steep vertical jumps also ensures steady ore flow.
