## Upgrader Routing: Strategic Placement and Multiplier Stacking

The most critical mistake new players make is arranging upgraders haphazardly. Upgraders generally fall into two mathematical categories: **additive (flat increase)** and **multiplicative (percentage or multiplier increase)**. Placing a 3x multiplier *before* a +50 flat value booster results in significantly lower payouts than doing the reverse.

### The Correct Mathematical Order

If an ore starts with a base value of $10:
* **Incorrect Sequence:** Base ($10) × Multiplier (3x) = $30 ➔ Flat Booster (+$50) = **$80 Final Value**
* **Correct Sequence:** Base ($10) + Flat Booster (+$50) = $60 ➔ Multiplier (3x) = **$180 Final Value**

By simply placing flat additions ahead of multipliers, you more than double the item yield without spending extra funds. As you acquire exponential multipliers, this gap widens drastically.

## Conveyor Physics and Ore Management Techniques

In TBOD environments, physics glitches and ore despawns are your greatest adversaries. Most engines assign a despawn timer—often between 30 and 60 seconds—to prevent server lag from lingering physical objects. If your track is overly convoluted, ores will vanish before reaching the collector, leaving you with zero income.

### Building Secure Corners and Elevation

Centrifugal force can cause fast-moving ores to slide off conveyor belts at sharp 90-degree corners. To prevent accidental losses, incorporate physical guide walls or use angled conveyor pieces that decelerate the item slightly before turning.

```
[Conveyor Straight] ➔ [Deceleration Pad] ➔ [Angled Wall / Guide] ➔ [Next Upgrader]
```

When building elevation changes, avoid steep inclines. Steep ramps often cause ores to tumble backward, creating bottlenecks that trigger cleanup scripts.

### Common Layout Issues and Practical Solutions

| Issue Encountered | Root Cause | Immediate Fix | Long-Term Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ore Despawning** | Conveyor loop exceeds item lifespan | Shorten travel path to collector | Upgrade conveyor speed modules |
| **Corner Derailment** | Velocity too high for turn angle | Place low-friction guide fences | Implement banked or curved belts |
| **Ore Stacking Jams** | Upgrader intake slower than belt speed | Add spacing regulators | Match dropper rate to upgrader capacity |
| **Missed Upgrades** | Ore passes over upgrader perimeter | Center conveyor alignment | Use narrowing funnels before gates |

Veteran players emphasize that refined routing outperforms raw length every time. Maintaining stability ensures continuous cash generation without sudden income drops.

Because you only have one dropper, passing an ore through an upgrader a single time limits your potential. Many game variants allow items to pass through select upgraders multiple times before collecting, provided they do not exceed processing caps.

### Constructing a Controlled Loop Track

A controlled loop diverts ores back through a series of multi-use upgraders before venting them to the furnace.

```
       ┌─────────── [Secondary Multiplier Loop] ──────────┐
       ▼                                                 │
[Dropper] ➔ [Initial Flat Boost] ➔ [Switch/Piston Gate] ─┴─► [Collector]
```

1. **The Entry Gate:** Raw ores enter from the dropper and receive baseline flat upgrades.

3. **The Exit Threshold:** Once the item reaches the maximum allowable loops or value cap, the gate routes it into the furnace.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most effective tbod tycoon but one dropper tricks for beginners?
The most effective trick for beginners is adhering strictly to upgrader math: place all flat cash additions directly adjacent to your dropper, followed by percentage and multiplier units further down the line. Additionally, keep your conveyor short early on to prevent items from despawning before they reach the furnace.

### Why do my ores keep disappearing before reaching the collector?
Most tycoon games have built-in despawn timers to minimize server memory usage. If your conveyor track is too long or has slow elevation transitions, the ore expires mid-run. Shorten your conveyor network, remove unnecessary loops, or upgrade your conveyor belt speed to resolve the problem.

### Should I prioritize dropper speed or upgrader multipliers first?
In single-dropper tycoons, upgrader multipliers provide vastly superior returns compared to dropper speed. While dropping ores faster helps slightly, multiplying the value of an existing ore by 5x or 10x yields exponential cash gains without overcrowding your conveyor belt.

### How do I prevent ores from flying off conveyor corners?
To stop high-speed items from derailing, install containment fences along corners, replace sharp 90-degree turns with smoother angled segments, and avoid putting sudden downward slopes directly before a turn.
