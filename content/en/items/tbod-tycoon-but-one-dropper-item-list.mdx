## Core Architecture: Understanding How TBOD Items Work

In Tycoon But One Dropper (TBOD), the gameplay loop shifts entirely from volume expansion to mathematical optimization. A standard factory builds wide; a single-dropper factory builds deep. The singular dispenser produces a baseline ore at fixed intervals, meaning your entire income curve hinges on how many multiplier thresholds that specific ore passes through before reaching the terminal collector.

Items in TBOD generally fall into five distinct operational classes:

1. **The Starter Dropper:** The permanent heart of your factory. While you cannot purchase additional dispensers, you can upgrade the base ore tier, drop rate frequency, and intrinsic ore properties.
2. **Processors and Multipliers:** Static gates, lasers, chemical vats, and polishers that apply flat additions or multiplicative boosts to passing ores.
3. **Conveyor Modules & Logistics:** Basic belts, angled turns, speed tracks, and elevated lifts that transport material through your factory floor.
4. **Looping & Reset Mechanisms:** Advanced routing tools such as directional splitters, teleporters, and condition-based resetters that allow ores to pass through upgraders multiple times without premature vaporization.
5. **Collectors and Vault Furnaces:** The finish line of your production line, converting processed ores into usable currency while granting bonus payout modifiers.

Understanding the interaction between these modules is critical. Many upgraders apply "tags" or temperature changes to ores. If an ore becomes overheated or carries conflicting modifiers, subsequent processing gates may destroy it entirely.

## Best Layout Strategies Using the TBOD Item Catalog

Even with every high-tier component unlocked from the **tbod tycoon but one dropper item list**, an unoptimized layout will severely bottleneck your income. In single-dropper gameplay, spatial management and order of operations reign supreme.

### 1. The Strict Multiplier Hierarchy
Always arrange your upgrader catalog following the rule of mathematical precedence:
- **Phase 1: Base Additions.** Apply all flat currency bonuses (+$10, +$50, +$250) immediately upon ore generation. Adding flat values after massive multipliers wastes potential gains.
- **Phase 2: Low-Tier Multipliers.** Route ores through low-magnitude multipliers (1.5x to 3.0x) that have minimal tag penalties.
- **Phase 3: Condition Enablers.** Apply thermal, chemical, or electrical tags required by advanced late-game processors.
- **Phase 4: High-Yield Resonators.** Send the tagged ore through heavy multipliers (5x to 15x) that scale off previously established tags.
- **Phase 5: Final Collection.** Dump into a furnace that offers a finishing multiplier rather than a standard flat deposit box.

### 2. Avoiding Hitbox Skips
When running high-speed conveyor belts, ores can travel too quickly across physical collision boxes. If an upgrader’s detection field is thin, high-velocity ores may pass clean through without registering the upgrade. Use standard-speed belts inside the upgrader housing itself, and place high-speed belts strictly on the transit corridors between processing hubs.

### Why did my ore disappear inside an upgrader?
Ores vanish primarily due to opposing elemental tags, over-processing limits, or health decay. For example, passing an already burning ore through an extreme heat processor can incinerate it entirely. Always install cooling units or tag cleansers between harsh thermal upgraders.

### Are looping tracks better than long straight lines?
Yes. Space constraints make extremely long straight lines impractical in mid-to-late gameplay. Using teleporters, curved loops, and conditional filter gates lets you re-use powerful upgraders multiple times, achieving exponentially higher payouts in a fraction of the physical footprint.

### Should I prioritize dropper upgrades or new upgraders first?
In the early game, purchasing new upgraders offers much higher return on investment than dropper upgrades. Once you have a reliable multiplier gauntlet established, upgrading the dropper's base ore value multiplies the effectiveness of your entire factory setup.
