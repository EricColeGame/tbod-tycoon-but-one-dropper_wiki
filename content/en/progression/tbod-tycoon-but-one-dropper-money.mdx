## Upgrader Stacking and Conveyor Routing to Boost Earnings

The core puzzle of TBOD revolves around conveyor geometry and upgrader mechanics. Simply placing upgraders in a straight line rarely produces optimal returns. To supercharge your **tbod tycoon but one dropper money** output, build structured processing zones. Design your conveyor track in an S-curve or spiral formation. This layout maximizes the physical footprint of your base, allowing you to fit numerous refinement stages between the dropper outlet and the collection furnace.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to get tbod tycoon but one dropper money in the early game?
The fastest way to generate early cash is to immediately reinvest every dollar into flat-value boosters followed by a single 2x multiplier gate. Never hoard currency or spend on cosmetic base elements during the first few tiers; prioritize track length and basic upgrader stacking to increase the cash value of every drop.

### How do upgrader multipliers stack in TBOD?
In TBOD, multipliers stack multiplicatively rather than additively, provided the upgraders are distinct models. For example, passing an ore through a 2x gate and then a 3x gate results in a 6x total multiplier on the ore’s current value. Arranging flat value additions before multiplier gates yields the highest cash returns.

### Why did my ore disappear on the conveyor belt without giving money?
Ores typically disappear for three reasons: they fell off an unguarded curve, they exceeded the game's internal ore lifespan timer, or they passed through an unstable upgrader with an ore-destruction penalty. Add safety walls to your corners and ensure your track length does not exceed the ore despawn threshold.

### When should I rebirth to keep my money growth optimal?
You should rebirth as soon as the cost of your next meaningful upgrader takes longer to save for than rebuilding your entire base under the new multiplier. If your current cash generation plateaus and progress stalls, activating a rebirth will refresh your momentum and unlock higher income tiers.
