## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most common mistake with tbod tycoon but one dropper upgrades?
The most frequent misstep is investing heavily in percentage-based multiplier gates before establishing strong flat-value boosters. Percentage gates multiply current ore value; if your solo dropper outputs low-tier ore without prior base value enhancements, a 5x multiplier yields minimal return. Always upgrade base dropper stats and flat buffers first.

### When should I perform my first rebirth in TBOD?
Community reports suggest waiting until you can easily surpass the base rebirth threshold rather than resetting the moment you qualify. Ideally, push your earnings until you can afford the first few tiers of rebirth-exclusive upgrades immediately upon restarting your base, minimizing early-game downtime.

### How do I prevent ores from falling off the conveyor belt?
Ore drop-off usually happens when conveyor motor speeds exceed the turn handling of your layout or when ores pile up at narrow gates. To fix this, install raised guardrails along sharp corners, space out intense processing pads, and ensure conveyor speeds remain uniform throughout the entire loop.

### Does upgrading dropper speed reduce individual ore value?
No, dropper speed strictly dictates how frequently new mineral blocks spawn from your single chute. However, dropping ores too rapidly can overwhelm under-leveled conveyor tracks and cause despawns due to entity limits. Ensure your conveyor throughput matches your dropper's firing rate.
