## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest method to earn tbod tycoon but one dropper bits as a beginner?
The quickest route for beginners is focusing entirely on dropper cooldown reductions and flat-value heaters. ### Do my bits reset when I rebirth or prestige?
No. Unlike standard factory cash, which resets to zero whenever you trigger a rebirth or enter a new expansion tier, bits are classified as permanent progression tokens. Any bits stored in your account remain completely safe across all standard reset levels, allowing you to gradually purchase end-game passive perks.

### Why is my ore disappearing before reaching the bit collection hopper?
Every piece of ore possesses a hidden despawn timer designed to prevent server lag. If your conveyor design incorporates too many loops, tight switchbacks, or slow elevation climbs, the ore will exceed its lifespan and dissolve mid-transit. Streamline your track by removing redundant conveyor segments and installing velocity boosters along high-friction curves.

### Should I prioritize conveyor speed or upgrader multipliers first?
In the early phases, upgrader multipliers provide the most immediate benefit to your bank account. However, once your multipliers successfully double your ore value, shift your investments directly into conveyor speed. Increasing belt velocity allows processed ore to reach the furnace faster, accelerating your overall bit generation rate and preventing traffic jams on the line.
