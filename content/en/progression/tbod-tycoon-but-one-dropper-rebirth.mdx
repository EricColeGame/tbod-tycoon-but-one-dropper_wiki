### 1. Establish the Clean Line Baseline
Start each fresh cycle by purchasing the primary conveyor line and the nearest basic upgraders. Avoid buying cosmetic flooring, defensive barriers, or non-essential aesthetic items until your ore path is completely secured.

### 2. Prioritize Multiplicative Over Additive Upgraders
In the early seconds after a reset, additive pads (which add flat values like +$10 or +$50) provide immediate lift. However, switch to multiplicative gates (2x, 3x, or percentage amplifiers) as early as your cash flow permits. According to community reports, running additive gates before multiplicative ones yields significantly higher final values than running them in reverse.

### 3. Route Through Conditional Polishers
Ensure that your single line passes through status-inflicting equipment—such as fire, radiation, or plasma polishers—prior to sending ore through conditional multipliers that specifically require those statuses.

### 4. Hit the Exact Target and Reset Immediately
A critical mistake made by newcomers executing their **tbod tycoon but one dropper rebirth** run is hoarding surplus cash. Once you meet the required threshold for the next rebirth rank, initiate the reset right away. Hoarding excess currency past the target rarely yields proportional advantages compared to the compounding permanent boosts granted by the next tier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I perform my first tbod tycoon but one dropper rebirth?
You should execute your first reset the precise moment you reach the required cash threshold displayed on the rebirth interface. ### Why did my ore disappear before reaching the rebirth furnace?
Ores typically disappear for two reasons: collision physics or durability depletion. If your conveyor moves too fast around sharp 90-degree corners, the ore can get launched off the belt. Alternatively, using intense elemental upgraders (like acid or inferno processors) can reduce the ore's durability to zero if you have not balanced the track with structural shielding or durability upgrades.

### Do I lose all my conveyor equipment after a rebirth?
Yes, triggering a reset clears your active factory footprint, conveyor tracks, and standard cash balance. However, you permanently keep your rebirth tier, unlocked token perks, achievement rewards, and persistent dropper multipliers, allowing you to rebuild your optimized layout in a fraction of the time.

### How do I optimize upgrader order for the fastest progression?
The optimal layout rule is: Flat Additive Upgraders first, Elemental/Status Applicators second, Multiplicative Gates third, and Extreme Percentage Finishers last. Applying high-value percentage multipliers to an ore that already possesses an elevated base value generates drastically more revenue than applying multipliers to a low-tier raw drop.
