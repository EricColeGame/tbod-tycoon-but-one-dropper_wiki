## Optimal Conveyor Routing and Upgrader Stacking

Because TBOD restricts you to a single source chute, your conveyor route dictates 100% of your earnings. Careless placement leads to ore degradation, dropped frames, or missed trigger zones. Before sending an ore into the converter for **tbod tycoon but one dropper essence**, you must maximize its precursor value through deliberate upgrader ordering.

### The Multiplier Rule: Additive vs. Multiplicative

Upgraders generally fall into two categories:
* **Additive Enhancers:** Add flat numerical value to raw ore (e.g., +500 Value). These must always be positioned at the very beginning of the conveyor route right beneath the dropper spout.
* **Multiplicative Amplifiers:** Multiply the current ore value (e.g., 2.5x, 4.0x, or conditional 10x thermal boosts). These should only sit downstream after the ore has absorbed every flat additive boost available.

If you route an un-buffed ore directly into an expensive multiplicative gate, you waste the vast majority of its theoretical output. Furthermore, ensure that directional conveyors avoid sharp, unbanked right-angle turns where physical ore bounce can cause the entity to skip an upgrader's hit-box.

## Advanced Late-Game Strategies: Looping and Serpentine Tracks

Once players unlock advanced track pieces such as directional lifts, teleporters, and dual-rail diverters, the physical layout of your single dropper becomes an engineering puzzle. ### Closed-Loop Recycling vs. Linear Serpentine

A common dilemma in high-tier progression is whether to recycle a single ore through an upgrader multiple times or build an elongated serpentine line. Most upgrader units feature an internal "tag limit"—meaning an ore can only receive the buff from that specific machine a set number of times (typically 1 to 3 times) before the machine ignores it.

* **Linear Serpentine:** The safest, most consistent approach. You stack unique upgraders one after another across wide S-curves. It completely eliminates tag limit desyncs and guarantees maximum value extraction before the ore hits the essence transmuter.
* **Teleport Cycling:** Uses teleporters or gravity hoppers to re-route ores through multi-hit units. While compact, player experience indicates this layout is prone to ore clipping if server latency fluctuates.
