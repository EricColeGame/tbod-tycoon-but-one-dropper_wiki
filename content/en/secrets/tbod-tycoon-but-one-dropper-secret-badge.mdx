- **Corner Peeking:** By zooming your camera completely in and positioning your character against a corner with Shift Lock active, you can briefly glance through solid geometry to identify hidden rooms, secret buttons, or ladders behind decorative panels.
- **Conveyor Surfing:** In single-dropper tycoons, conveyor belts exert continuous directional force. You can use this momentum to fling your character toward far-off boundary ledges by timing a jump right as your character reaches the edge of a high-speed belt.
- **Shadow Detection:** Look for subtle lighting breaks. Dynamic shadows in engine rendering often cast dark lines where two separate parts meet. If a wall is entirely solid, the shadow remains uniform; if an illusory doorway exists, faint shadow seams often outline the entrance.
